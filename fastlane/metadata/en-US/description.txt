Study soccer’s 17 laws with an independent refereeing course. The included 2026/27 guide pairs each law with a game situation so you can practice applying what you’ve read. Explore the daily law, search the rulebook, bookmark chapters, and mark them as studied.

Practice with five randomly selected situations or review calls you got wrong. Get immediate feedback and an explanation after each practice answer. Take three untimed seasonal exam blocks with an 80% passing score for each. Pass all three in the current season to generate a personalized PDF certificate for your learning records.

Track your study streak, progress, and exam history in My course. The included guide, practice, exams, and progress work offline. You can also import a copy of the official IFAB PDF for offline reading and transfer course progress with a JSON file.

Available on iPhone and iPad with iOS or iPadOS 17.4 or later. The interface and course are available in English, German, Spanish, Italian, Brazilian Portuguese, and French.
