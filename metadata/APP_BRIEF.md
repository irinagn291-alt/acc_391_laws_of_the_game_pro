# Laws of the Game

## What it is

An independent football rules course for aspiring and practicing referees, coaches, players, and anyone who wants to understand match decisions. It pairs 17 chapters of study notes with match situations, an untimed seasonal exam, and a personal course certificate.

## Launch and onboarding

1. A cold launch opens the “Learn” tab under the title “Laws of the Game”. There is no onboarding sequence or first-launch permission prompt.
2. The first screen shows “FOOTBALL • 2026/27” and a two-line headline, “Know the game.” followed by “Make the call.” The “Rule of the day” card shows the day’s two-digit law number and chapter title. Tap “Explore this law” to open that chapter.
3. “Your referee journey” starts at “0 / 17” for “Chapters”, “0 / 17” for “Decisions”, and “0 / 3” for “Exam blocks”. Below it, “A moment. A decision.” shows “01 — 17” and “Work through match situations and understand the reason behind every call.” Tap “Start practice” to switch to “Practice”.
4. The tab bar shows “Learn”, “Rulebook”, “Practice”, and “My course”. Each tab has a top-right sliders icon with the accessibility label “Settings”.

## Screens

### “Learn”

- The navigation title is “Laws of the Game”. The “Rule of the day” card, including “Explore this law”, opens a rotating chapter selected from the 17 laws by the device’s calendar date.

### “Rulebook”

- “All chapters” shows the full index. “Saved laws” limits it to bookmarked chapters. “Search laws and explanations” filters the current selection by chapter title and study text.
- Each chapter row opens its study notes. Rows show the two-digit law number, title, a preview of the notes, and icons for studied and bookmarked chapters where applicable.

### Chapter study

- The chapter opens full-screen. Its heading combines the law number and fixed edition, for example “LAW 11 • 2026/27”, followed by the chapter title, a pitch illustration, and selectable text under “Study notes”.
- “Mark as studied” adds the chapter to progress and records study activity for the day. The label changes to “Studied”. Tapping it again records activity for the day without adding a second chapter or removing the studied mark.
- The toolbar bookmark icon has the accessibility label “Saved laws”. Tap it to add or remove the chapter from that list. The icon fills when saved.
- “Try a decision” opens the one practice question for that law. “Close” returns to the originating tab.
- The independent learning guide note appears below the controls. “Official source · online” opens the corresponding law on IFAB’s site.

### “Official edition” PDF reader

- “Search laws and explanations” is the search field. Submit from the keyboard or tap the unlabeled circled right-arrow button to jump to the first case-insensitive match in the imported PDF.
- The unlabeled left and right chevrons step through pages. The counter uses “Page” followed by the current and total page numbers, for example “Page 1 / 17”. The PDF also supports native scrolling and zoom.
- “PDF contents” opens a sheet with the PDF’s own outline labels where available. For PDFs without an outline, it lists entries such as “Page 1”. Tapping an entry jumps to that page and closes the sheet. “Close” dismisses the contents sheet or, in the reader toolbar, returns to “Rulebook”.

## Behaviours that can look like bugs

- **Empty search or bookmarks.** “No chapters found” and “Save a chapter with the bookmark button to find it here.” appear for any empty results, including an unmatched search in “All chapters”. Clear the search, switch to “All chapters”, or bookmark a chapter to populate “Saved laws”.
- **An answer is required.** “Next situation” and “Finish” appear only after selecting an answer. A practice choice is final for that question. In an exam, the choice can be changed until “Next situation” or “Finish” is tapped. “Save and close” keeps submitted answers. A highlighted choice on the current question must be selected again on resume if it was never submitted.
- **The mistakes list changes with practice.** “Review mistakes” is disabled when there are no current mistakes. The message is “No mistakes to review yet. Start a practice session.” An incorrect practice answer adds a question. Answering it correctly later removes it. The “Decisions” total counts each question ever answered correctly in practice once. Exam answers affect exam results separately.
- **One exam at a time.** While a block is unfinished, all three block cards are disabled. Use “Resume exam” and finish it to make them available again. After “Keep learning”, close the result and tap the block again to retry. Completed attempts add to “Exam history”, including failed attempts. Before the first one, it says “Your completed exam blocks will appear here.”
- **Season rollover.** The current exam season changes on 1 July. Current-season passes are counted anew, older completed results stay in “Exam history”, and an unfinished prior-season block is discarded on a fresh launch. The study edition label stays “2026/27”.
- **Certificate locked or button disabled.** “Pass every exam block to unlock your certificate.” clears after passing all three current-season blocks at 80% or higher. That means at least 5 correct out of 6 in “Foundations”, 4 out of 5 in “Reading the game”, and 5 out of 6 in “Making the call”. “Create PDF” stays disabled until “Name on certificate” contains a non-whitespace character. Generate the PDF to reveal the preview and share control. Reopening the sheet retains the name and requires generating the preview again.
- **No imported PDF yet.** “Read imported PDF” is hidden until a successful import. If import fails, the alert says “This PDF could not be opened. Choose an unlocked PDF under 40 MB.” Tap “Close” and use “Import your PDF” with a readable, unlocked PDF under 40 MB containing at least one page. Reader arrows stop at the first and last page. A search without a match leaves the reader in place.
- **Reminder switches may stay off.** When iOS denies notification access, enabling a reminder fails. Use “Open system settings” to allow notifications, then enable the reminder. The course can be used with both switches off.
- **The file controls expect different documents.** “Import your PDF” is for reading an official-edition document. “Import file” in “Settings” expects a compatible course-progress export. Use “Export file” to create one.
- **Saving can fail.** If the app shows “Could not save changes. Free some device storage and try again.”, free storage and repeat the action.

## Starter content and resume

The app ships with 17 study chapters and 17 three-choice match situations, one per law. A new learner starts with empty bookmarks, practice outcomes, exam history, and certificate name, and no imported official PDF.

One unfinished current-season exam can resume across launches through “Resume exam”. Its question order and submitted answers are retained. Free practice, mistake review, and individual chapter sessions start fresh when reopened, although answers already tapped in practice count toward the course record. The app opens on “Learn” on a normal cold launch.

## Permissions

Notifications are the only permission requested. iOS asks for alerts, sounds, and badges when the learner first tries to enable “Rule of the day” or “New season reminder” in “Settings”. The prompt uses system wording and there is no custom usage-description string. Document import and export use the system picker for files the learner selects.

## Absent

- Login or app accounts.
- In-app purchases.
- Ads.
- Analytics.
- User-generated content creation or publishing features. The app accepts a private certificate name and imports user-selected documents.
- Account deletion flow. “Reset local progress” clears the device’s course record.
- App Tracking Transparency prompt.

## Data and support

Study progress, bookmarks, practice outcomes, exam attempts, reminder preferences, the certificate name, and imported PDFs are kept on the device. Learners can move course progress manually with “Export file” and “Import file”, and can send a generated certificate through the system share sheet. An exported progress file excludes the active exam, certificate name, imported PDF, and reminder preferences. This build has no in-app iCloud sync control or developer service for transferring study data.

There is no in-app “Contact” or “Support” control. “Official source · online” is a rules reference linking to IFAB.

## Platform

- Native iPhone and iPad app. Minimum iOS or iPadOS version is 17.4.
- iPhone supports portrait and landscape in both directions. iPad also supports upside-down portrait.
- The course has no region gate. By default it uses the first supported device language, with Portuguese mapped to Brazilian Portuguese and English as the fallback. A manual “Language” selection takes precedence. Daily rotation, season rollover, and reminders use the device’s calendar.
- The English Home Screen name is “Laws of the Game”. The localized Home Screen names are “Fußballregeln”, “Reglas del fútbol”, “Regole del calcio”, “Regras do futebol”, and “Lois du football”. The first tab’s navigation title stays “Laws of the Game” across languages.
